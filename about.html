Product proof

A working product, not a pitch deck.

JobDetector combines source-of-truth job collection with a personal recommendation layer. It is built for job seekers who want signal, speed, and control instead of stale listings and duplicate search results.

Direct-source job data

The system collects roles from company ATS and career pages, reducing recruiter reposts and stale aggregator noise.

Personal Job Radar

Users can create career directions, add resume signals, set geography, exclude poor-fit roles, and rank recent openings.

Feedback-driven workflow

Favorites, saved searches, company requests, recommendation feedback, and digest settings create a loop for product learning.

Founder note

Why I built it.

I built JobDetector because I was frustrated with job boards that miss high-quality roles, show stale postings, and bury useful signals behind keyword search. Every crawler, profile system, ranking rule, digest workflow, and UI iteration has been built and tested directly through my own job search workflow. The product is intentionally practical: find the right openings earlier, understand why they match, and keep improving the recommendation loop.
